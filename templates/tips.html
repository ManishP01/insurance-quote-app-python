Bundle Your Policies

High Impact Low Effort

Combine auto, home, and life insurance with the same provider

  • Save 10-25% on premiums
  • Simplified billing and management
  • Better customer service priority
  • Potential for additional loyalty discounts

Increase Your Deductible

Medium Impact Low Effort

Higher deductibles mean lower monthly premiums

  • Increasing from $500 to $1000 can save 10-15%
  • Make sure you can afford the higher deductible
  • Consider your emergency fund before deciding
  • Best for drivers with good safety records

Improve Your Credit Score

High Impact High Effort

Better credit often leads to better insurance rates

  • Pay bills on time consistently
  • Keep credit utilization below 30%
  • Don't close old credit accounts
  • Monitor your credit report regularly

Take a Defensive Driving Course

Medium Impact Medium Effort

Complete an approved safety course for discounts

  • Usually saves 5-10% on premiums
  • Courses available online or in-person
  • May need to retake every few years
  • Some states mandate this discount
